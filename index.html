Media software

The reel is one half of this page. The software behind media is the other.

In development

Designed here. Built on open foundations.

A media-center system inspired by XBMC.

Alongside the daily reel, the founder is building a media-center system of his own design — his configuration and his library standards throughout — on an open-source platform foundation. The inspiration is XBMC. The goal is library-grade organization, with games, video, TV, and music each kept to one standard.

The system is still in development, so this page says what it is for and stops there. When there is more to show, it will be published with its record attached.
